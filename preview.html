🌟 Project Spotlight

💻 macOS-web (DeskTab) — Desktop OS in Your Browser

Turn any browser new-tab into a sleek, customizable MacBook workspace complete with native-feeling dock, widgets, and app launcher.

  • 🍏 Authentic macOS Interface: Floating menu bar, interactive system clock, battery indicators, and a responsive magnifying dock.
  • ⚡ Spotlight & Quick Launch: Rapid search bar, quick shortcuts to top web platforms, and integrated AI tool launcher panels.
  • 🎨 Total Personalization: Custom high-res wallpapers, dynamic video backgrounds, movable widgets, and personalized themes.
  • 🔒 Zero Tracking / Pure Performance: Built without heavy framework overhead — lightweight, instant boot, and completely private.
